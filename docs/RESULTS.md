## 2026-10-20 — Hawaii annual CAPN run (531ee66)
**What was run:** Annual Hawaii bigeye CAPN valuation snapshots for 2010–2018 using the R `capn` package.
**Key settings:** 3% discount rate; alpha = 1, gamma_hawaii = 0, beta_other = 1; common fitted logistic growth parameters r ≈ 0.4816/year and K ≈ 1,205,670 tonnes.
**Results:** Shadow price at assessed stock ranged from $215.26 to $324.36 per tonne; capitalization ranged from $124.2 million to $186.1 million.
**Conclusion:** These are working results conditional on proxy geographic allocations and provisional behavioral assumptions; they need review before being treated as final estimates.
**Artifacts:** `results/hawaii_capn_annual/annual_valuation.csv` and five figure PDFs in `results/hawaii_capn_annual/`.
